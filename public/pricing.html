Pricing Questions

Why do prices vary between neighbors?

Two lawns on the same street can take very different time. Fence lines, play sets, slopes, and edging footage all change the work, so they change the price.

Is there a discount for bundling services?

Yes. Weekly mowing customers get preferred pricing on cleanups, mulch, and trimming because we're already on the property.

Do you require a season-long contract?

No. We keep customers by doing good work, not by locking them in.
